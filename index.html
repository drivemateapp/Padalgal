PADALGAL Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 11, 2026

PADALGAL (“the App”) is an offline music player developed by Manikandan Ramesh. This Privacy Policy explains how PADALGAL handles user information, device permissions and locally stored music.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

PADALGAL does not require users to create an account or provide personal information.

The App does not intentionally collect, sell or share:

  • Names
  • Email addresses
  • Phone numbers
  • Contacts
  • Location information
  • Login credentials
  • Payment information
  • Personal music files
  • Audio recordings
  • Advertising identifiers

2. Music and Media Files

PADALGAL accesses audio files stored locally on the user's device only to provide music playback functionality.

The App does not upload music files to a server or automatically share them online.

3. Device Permissions

PADALGAL may request permission to access audio files or media stored on the device. These permissions are used to discover, display and play local music files and support library-related features.

4. Locally Stored Application Data

PADALGAL may store information locally on the device to support:

  • Favorites
  • Playlists
  • Recently played songs
  • Most played counts
  • Queue information
  • Equalizer preferences
  • Sleep timer preferences
  • Application settings

This information is not automatically transmitted to the developer.

5. Internet and Online Services

PADALGAL is designed as an offline-first application and does not provide online music streaming, cloud music backup, advertising-based tracking or behavioral profiling.

6. Sharing Features

If the user explicitly uses Android's sharing functionality, selected content may be passed to the sharing application chosen by the user. PADALGAL does not automatically share content without user action.

7. Third-Party Services

PADALGAL does not intentionally integrate advertising SDKs, tracking SDKs or third-party analytics services for collecting user behavior. Android system components and required libraries may have their own terms and policies.

8. Data Deletion

Users can delete locally stored application data by clearing the App's storage through Android Settings or by uninstalling PADALGAL. This may remove playlists, favorites, play counts and preferences.

9. Children's Privacy

PADALGAL does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when application functionality or data practices change. Updated versions will be published at this URL.
